# Part 1: Demo video

In this part, you have to present your project and the advancements in 5-minutes video.

The video should NOT contain any sounds. Information should be conveyed using animations, subtitles, or visual highlights.

**For this continuous assessment, the video should be made public (e.g., uploaded on YouTube) and the link to the video must be provided on TOMUSS.**

You are free to choose the structure of your video depending on your project type. For example, in the case of a prototype tool, the video should include the following elements:

- **Introduction:** Introduce the concept of your project.
- **Aim:** What is the purpose of your project?
- **Main features:** Present the system’s main features and discuss the benefits as well as innovations.
- **Development:** Architecture overview, agent-based techniques, and technologies used for development.
- **Demo.** Execution of the system that highlights its features and benefits.

The video should start with the name of your project, the date, the name of the course, and the name of the participating students.
